### Generated Triangle-Center Recovery

Global refinement on the discrete surface produced 1,082 new nodes at the
origin. A literal parent-triangle/fine-three-Q-star audit independently
identified exactly those 1,082 generated face centers. They were restored
to their original linear triangle's arithmetic center; no original node,
other generated coordinate or original surface triangle was changed.

After that analytic restoration, 790 cells had all sampled determinants
negative. Reflecting only their H8 slot order restored orientation without
changing geometry; mixed-sign cells were never reflected as a false repair.

The resulting 370,528 H8/397,757-node diagnostic has:

- zero nonmanifold, twisted or incorrectly directed shared faces;
- all 13,222 outer cycles matched to their literal source-parent partitions;
- all 2,160 root fine9 and 334 head-cap fine4 interfaces preserved;
- volume `8.684993327409588 mm^3`, matching the skin/mixed volume to roundoff;
- **two remaining negative-corner-J cells**, although their Gauss samples
  and integrated volumes are positive;
- very poor shape outliers, including maximum edge ratio about 980.

It is therefore **rejected**, with no passing core K/INP exported. Recovery
and refinement experiments remain in scratch rather than a promoted native
production generator. The next quality repair must not move the protected
skin, relax positivity or hide those shape outliers.
