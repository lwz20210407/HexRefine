# Sampling Limits

The names/descriptions inside harvested JSON files are historical evidence,
not a certificate that their cells cover a complete parent replacement domain.

In particular, `trbb_valence3_absorber_rectilinear.json` (SHA-256
`9a3923808050869a66df7df2bd146b36c317cec04ef4558d680a0c6b947782b8`)
is **not a complete three-parent nine-Hex macro**. Rational volume/face audit
on2026-10-21 found65.625% coverage of the three natural complete parent columns,
only3 of12 required fine top quads, and15 sampled free faces inside that macro.
Positive local Jacobians and a closed boundary of the sampled subvolume do
not establish complete coverage. Keep the original sample unchanged for
comparison; do not register those nine cells as an in-place macro.

This does not establish cracks in TrueGrid's complete source mesh: omitted
cells may supply the missing interfaces. A usable macro needs original parent
IDs/vertices, complete outer partitions, full volume/coverage and neighboring
field compatibility, not only cells touching a singular line.

See [full ROI scope and evidence](../OGRID_FULL_ROI.md) for currently verified
native routes and remaining limits.
